# RimworldMods
Rimworld mod projects.

AnimalsLogic - fixes some issues with animal behavior

Carbon - adds two new materials, carbon fiber (wood/metal type) and diamond (stone type)

HousekeeperAssistanceCat - a new animal that can be trained to do hauling and simple cleaning jobs

Leeani - alien race of fox people

MyLittlePlanet - allows making smaller worlds

BetterCoolers16, BetterVents16 - 1.6/1.7 updates of BetterCoolers and BetterVents: coolers and vents placed inside walls

e71ImportantClean - cleaners clean the rooms where cleanliness matters (kitchens, hospitals, bedrooms...) first

VandalHeartsChase - Prepare Carefully world pawns join friendly factions and turn up with visitors and traders

VandalHeartsLegacy - events and quests that bring those characters to your colony (needs VandalHeartsChase)
